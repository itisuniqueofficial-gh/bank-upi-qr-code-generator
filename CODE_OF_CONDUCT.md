# Code of Conduct

The canonical Code of Conduct lives at
**[`.github/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`](.github/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)** — that copy is
what GitHub surfaces in the repository's **Community** tab.

This root file is kept in sync with it for visibility. It is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1
([read it online](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html)).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience
for everyone, and to act in ways that foster an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

**Positive behavior:**

- Empathy, kindness, and respect for differing opinions
- Graceful acceptance of constructive feedback
- Accountability and learning from mistakes
- Focus on the community as a whole

**Unacceptable behavior:**

- Sexualized language or imagery, sexual attention/advances
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Any conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Community leaders will review and investigate reports of unacceptable
behavior promptly and fairly. Consequences follow the Contributor Covenant's
enforcement ladder: **correction → warning → temporary ban → permanent ban**.

## Reporting

Report instances of abuse, harassment, or other unacceptable behavior to the
maintainers privately (see
[.github/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](.github/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md#enforcement) for
details). All reports are kept confidential.
