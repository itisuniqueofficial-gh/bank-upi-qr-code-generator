How it works

The QR code encodes a UPI deep link like upi://pay?pa=…&pn=…&am=…&cu=INR. Scan it with any UPI app such as Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm to open the payment screen. Use the account IFSC code from your bank statement or net banking.

All processing happens in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, tracked, or stored on a server. The app also works offline once loaded.

About UPI QR Code Generator

About

UPI QR Code Generator builds a scannable upi://pay QR code from your bank account number and IFSC code. It is free, open source, and runs 100% in your browser.

Privacy

Your bank details are processed locally on this device. Nothing you type is uploaded, tracked, or stored on a server. Form values are saved only in your browser's local storage so you can resume later, and are cleared when you press Reset.
