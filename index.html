How it works

The QR code encodes a UPI deep link like upi://pay?pa=…&pn=…&am=…&cu=INR. Scan it with any UPI app such as Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm to open the payment screen. Use the account IFSC code from your bank statement or net banking.

All processing happens in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, tracked, or stored on a server. The app also works offline once loaded.
